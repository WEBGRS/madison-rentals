# Madison campus rentals map

A map of rentals within two miles of the UW–Madison campus: 605 properties, 2,988 units or floor plans, 67 landlords, collected on 2026-10-01.

**Open the map: https://webgrs.github.io/madison-rentals/**

Listings were gathered from landlord websites and the UW Off-Campus Housing service, then merged. Every value says where it came from (landlord site, UW list, read from the description, or not stated), and the *Missing info* tab lists what each landlord has not published, with a ready-to-send email draft.

Zoom in to see Madison Metro bus stops and their routes. Under *My places* you can save the places you go often (lab, gym, job) and see each rental's straight-line distance to them; those places stay in your own browser.

Prices and availability change quickly. Treat this as a starting point and confirm details with the landlord before you sign anything.
